Responsibilities
- An operator that considers or has reason to believe food it imported, produced, processed, manufactured or distributed is non-compliant must immediately initiate withdrawal procedures and inform the competent authorities when the food has left that operator’s immediate control. (Regulation 178/2002, Article 19 [Ee982cc4f6e790be7])
  Condition: The duty described applies when the operator considers or has reason to believe the food is not in compliance with food-safety requirements and it has left the initial operator’s immediate control.
- If the product may have reached consumers, the operator must effectively and accurately inform them of the reason for withdrawal; it must recall products already supplied to them if necessary because other measures are insufficient for a high level of health protection. (Regulation 178/2002, Article 19 [Ee982cc4f6e790be7])
  Condition: Consumer information and recall are conditional on the product possibly having reached consumers; recall is required if necessary and other measures are insufficient.
- When non-compliance is established, competent authorities must determine its origin and extent and the operator’s responsibilities, and take appropriate measures to remedy it and prevent recurrence. (Regulation 2017/625, Article 138 [E4110caee057f6f3f])
  Condition: This applies where the competent authorities have established non-compliance; the measures must take account of its nature and the operator’s compliance record.
