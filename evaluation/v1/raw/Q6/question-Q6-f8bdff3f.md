Direct answer
- If a consignment entering the Union is non-compliant with the applicable rules, the competent authorities must place it under official detention and refuse entry. They must order the operator responsible for the consignment to destroy it, re-dispatch it outside the Union, or subject it to special treatment or another measure necessary to ensure compliance. (Regulation 2017/625, Article 66 / part 1 of 2 [E39fe50bc4d640a7f])
  Condition: Applies to a non-compliant consignment entering the Union; the appropriate action depends on the circumstances and applicable rules.

Legal requirements
- Before ordering one of those measures, the competent authority must hear the operator concerned, unless immediate action is necessary to respond to a specified risk. (Regulation 2017/625, Article 66 / part 1 of 2 [E39fe50bc4d640a7f])
  Condition: Applies to an order under Article 66(3), subject to the immediate-action exception stated in that provision.
- The competent authorities must immediately notify the Commission, other Member States' competent authorities, customs authorities, the competent authorities of the third country of origin, and the operator responsible for the consignment of an entry refusal and covered orders. (Regulation 2017/625, Article 66 / part 1 of 2 [E39fe50bc4d640a7f])
  Condition: Applies to entry refusals under Article 66(1) and orders covered by Article 66(5).
- Examples of measures competent authorities may choose include restricting or prohibiting placing goods on the market or their movement, and ordering recall, withdrawal, removal, or destruction of goods. (Regulation 2017/625, Article 138 [E4110caee057f6f3f])
  Condition: These are examples of measures available under Article 138(2), not measures that must all be imposed in this case.
- For an entering non-compliant consignment, the operator responsible for the consignment must carry out measures ordered by the competent authorities without delay and no later than 60 days after notification, unless the authority specifies a shorter period. (Regulation 2017/625, Article 69 [Ea36a9c8d394c1a05])
  Condition: Applies to measures ordered under the provisions cited in Article 69(1); a shorter deadline may be set by the competent authorities.
- Measures under Article 66 are at the expense of the operator responsible for the consignment. (Regulation 2017/625, Article 66 / part 2 of 2 [E64775249415899a5])
  Condition: Applies to measures referred to in Article 66.
- A food business operator who considers or has reason to believe that food it imported, produced, processed, manufactured, or distributed is not compliant with food-safety requirements must immediately initiate withdrawal procedures if the food has left that operator's immediate control, and inform the competent authorities. (Regulation 178/2002, Article 19 [Ee982cc4f6e790be7])
  Condition: Applies when the operator has the stated belief or reason to believe and the food has left the initial operator's immediate control.

Responsibilities
- Once non-compliance is established, competent authorities must determine its origin and extent and establish the operator's responsibilities, then take appropriate measures to ensure the operator remedies it and prevents recurrence. (Regulation 2017/625, Article 138 [E4110caee057f6f3f])
  Condition: The authority must take account of the nature of the non-compliance and the operator's past compliance record.
- Each Member State designates the competent authority or authorities responsible for organising or performing official controls and other official activities in the relevant area. (Regulation 2017/625, Article 4 [E840781f71418198b])
